2. STUDY OVERVIEW
-----------------
This repository contains the environmental, morphological, and molecular datasets, as well as the statistical R scripts, supporting the study on how local management protection levels, anthropogenic pressure (Human Influence Index, HII), sediment characteristics, and morphodynamic beach zonation shape sandy beach meiofaunal communities across the Balearic Islands. 

Quantitative morphological samples were collected using 6.8 cm inner-diameter corers across three morphodynamic zones (swash, shoaling, and subtidal) at nine beaches spanning a gradient of local management protection. Molecular community composition was assessed via 18S rRNA (V1–V2 hypervariable region) metabarcoding.
